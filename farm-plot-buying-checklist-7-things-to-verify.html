Farm Plot Buying Checklist: 7 Things To Verify Before You Pay

A comprehensive 7-point due diligence roadmap to verify ownership title, Dharani land records, physical survey boundaries, and hidden costs before paying an advance for farmland near Hyderabad.

Varalakshmi Meduri
Managing Director • Vara Real Estates
October 27, 2026

Introduction

Buying a farm plot can be an exciting step toward owning land, enjoying a greener lifestyle, or exploring long-term property investment opportunities near Hyderabad. However, purchasing farmland requires careful research and proper verification before making any financial commitment. A property's attractive price, location, or promised amenities should never replace a thorough review of its legal status and physical condition.

Whether you are a first-time buyer or an experienced investor, following a Farm Plot Buying Checklist can help you identify potential risks and make a more informed decision.

Before paying a booking amount or advance, take time to verify the ownership documents, land classification, access roads, plot boundaries, available facilities, and complete purchase costs. These seven checks can help you approach your farmland purchase with greater confidence.

1

Verify Land Ownership And Legal Documents

The first step is confirming that the seller has the legal right to sell the property. Request the registered sale deed, previous ownership documents, and relevant revenue records. Review the chain of ownership to identify gaps, inheritance issues, or inconsistencies.

Obtain an Encumbrance Certificate (EC) for an appropriate period and have a qualified property lawyer review it. An EC can reveal certain registered transactions and liabilities, but it does not independently establish that a property is free from every legal dispute.

If you are considering Farm Plots For Sale Near Hyderabad, verify the survey number, extent of land, seller's ownership details, and applicable Telangana land records. Also check for pending litigation, government restrictions, mortgages, and claims by other parties.

Do not rely solely on photocopies, verbal assurances, or documents supplied by a salesperson. Independent verification is essential before transferring money.

Key Documents to Request & Verify

Step 1 Audit
Registered Sale Deed (Mother Deed) Traces past 30-year unbroken chain of title transfers.
Encumbrance Certificate (EC) Issued by Telangana Sub-Registrar confirming registered encumbrances.
Pattadar Passbook & Dharani Records Confirms land ownership, mutation entries, and khata numbers.
Court Dispute & Bank Mortgage Clearance Independent search against civil suits, land ceiling, or bank liens.

Non-Negotiable Legal Warning

Never accept photocopied sale deeds or verbal guarantees from intermediaries. Always cross-check the survey number against the Dharani land portal and obtain a formal Title Search Report prepared by a practicing revenue advocate.

2

Check Land Classification And Required Approvals

Not every agricultural plot can legally be used for residential construction or farmhouse development. The permitted use depends on the property's classification, applicable laws, local planning regulations, and required permissions.

Before buying, establish whether the land is agricultural, converted for another use, or part of an approved plotted development. Ask for relevant conversion orders, layout permissions, and other approvals where applicable.

If you plan to build a farmhouse, confirm the specific construction requirements with the appropriate authority. Purchasing agricultural land does not automatically provide permission to construct a residential building.

Buyers exploring Agricultural Land For Sale Near Hyderabad should also check whether the property falls under any restricted or prohibited land category. A qualified local lawyer can help determine whether the proposed purchase and intended use comply with applicable regulations.

Land Classification & Permitted Use Rules

Zoning & Sanctions
Section 22-A Prohibited Land List Confirm plot is not assigned government land, Wakf, Endowment, or forest buffer.
NALA / Land Conversion Order Verify if conversion from agriculture to non-agriculture has been processed.
Gram Panchayat / DTCP Layout Sanction Layouts with demarcated road widths, open spaces, and registered plot numbers.
Farmhouse Building Permissions Confirm setback requirements, permissible plinth area, and municipal clearances.
3

Inspect Plot Boundaries And Physical Measurements

The plot you see on-site should match the dimensions and location described in the official records. Ask for the survey map or relevant cadastral sketch and compare it with the physical boundaries.

Whenever possible, arrange an independent survey through a qualified surveyor. Verify the survey number, total extent, boundary markers, adjoining properties, and any overlapping claims.

Do not assume that a fence, boundary wall, or marking placed by a seller accurately represents the registered plot boundaries.

For buyers considering Farm Land Investment Near Hyderabad, a physical inspection can also reveal practical issues such as uneven terrain, drainage problems, waterlogging, or limited access. Visit the property personally and assess its condition before finalizing the deal.

A
Tippon & Village Map Matching

Cross-verify the village map (cadastral sketch) and Tippon with field measurements to ensure the plot is not located in a lake catchment area, stream buffer, or road-widening zone.

B
Four Boundary Clearances (Chakkubandi)

Confirm the East, West, North, and South boundary neighbors listed in the sale deed match the physical ground reality, preventing boundary disputes with adjacent landowners.

C
Topography & Monsoon Drainage

Inspect the land during or after rains if possible to identify low-lying depressions, waterlogging, steep slopes, or rocky outcrops that would increase future levelling costs.

4

Verify Road Access And Connectivity

Reliable road access is important whether you intend to cultivate the land, visit it on weekends, or consider a future permitted development.

Check the route from the nearest main road to the actual plot. Find out whether the approach road is usable throughout the year and whether there is a legally established right of access.

A road shown in a brochure or layout plan may not always provide unrestricted legal access. Confirm the relevant records and permissions rather than relying on verbal promises.

Also evaluate the distance from Hyderabad, approximate travel time, nearby villages, essential services, and public transport availability. Convenient connectivity can make the property more practical for regular visits.

Right-of-Way (R.O.W.) Verification: Ensure the approach road is an officially notified public cart-track or dedicated revenue road. Avoid landlocked plots accessible only via verbal permissions across neighboring agricultural fields, which can be blocked at any time.
5

Check Water, Electricity, And Available Amenities

Water availability is a major consideration when purchasing farmland. Vara Real Estates Ask whether the property has access to a borewell, well, irrigation source, or another reliable water supply. Where appropriate, verify water availability and quality independently.

Check electricity access, connection status, internal roads, drainage, fencing, and other facilities advertised by the seller. Clarify whether these amenities already exist or are only proposed for future development.

If you are evaluating Affordable Farm Plots Near Hyderabad, compare the facilities included in the price with any additional charges for development, maintenance, water, or electricity.

Do not assume that every amenity is included in the advertised price. Request written confirmation of promised facilities, completion timelines, and applicable maintenance responsibilities.

Utilities & Infrastructure Checklist

Operational Checks
Ground Water & Borewell Depth Check local groundwater levels, existing borewell discharge (inches of water), and water TDS for plantation.
Electricity Supply (3-Phase Power) Verify power line proximity, transformer capacity, and agricultural/domestic meter sanctions.
Perimeter Fencing & Security Verify solar perimeter fencing, CCTV surveillance, and 24/7 guarded entrance gate setup.
Internal Roads & Storm Drains Check internal avenue width (33 ft minimum), blacktop/gravel surface, and runoff drainage channels.
6

Calculate The Total Purchase Cost

The advertised price is only one part of the total amount you may need to pay. Before committing, prepare a complete cost estimate covering the plot price, stamp duty, registration charges, legal consultation, survey expenses, and any applicable development or maintenance fees.

Ask for an itemized quotation and review the payment schedule carefully. Understand the booking terms, cancellation policy, refund conditions, and deadlines before signing an agreement.

Avoid transferring money to an unrelated account or making payments without proper documentation and receipts. Ensure the recipient is authorized to receive the payment and that the terms are recorded in writing.

Comparing the complete cost of multiple properties can help you identify a suitable option without exceeding your budget.

Cost Component Description Payment Category
Base Plot Price Cost of land area (per gunta or square yard agreed in sale agreement). Mandatory
Stamp Duty & Registration Statutory Telangana registration charges and stamp duty paid at Sub-Registrar office. Statutory
Legal Due Diligence Fee Professional legal opinion and title verification fee paid to an independent advocate. Recommended
Survey & Demarcation Fee for independent licensed surveyor to verify boundaries and fix stone pegs. Recommended
Infrastructure & Amenities Charge Contribution toward internal blacktop roads, water lines, solar fencing, and landscaping. Layout Specific
Maintenance Deposit (CAM) Prepaid or annual fee covering farm security, water management, and plantation care. Ongoing

Financial Transparency Best Practice

Always request an official tax invoice and formal receipt for every rupee paid. Ensure all bank transfers are made directly to the developer's registered company bank account, never to personal accounts of sales associates.

7

Research The Seller And Review The Agreement

Before paying an advance, verify the seller's identity and authority to sell the property. If a developer or agent is involved, check the company's background, project documentation, completed developments, and the exact role of each party in the transaction.

Read the agreement carefully and confirm the plot identification, sale price, payment milestones, possession terms, promised amenities, and obligations of the seller. If the property is part of a plotted development, verify the relevant project approvals and RERA registration requirements, where applicable.

Never allow pressure to make you skip legal checks. If important documents are missing, boundaries are unclear, or the seller refuses independent verification, pause the transaction until the concerns are resolved.

Sale Agreement Clauses to Inspect

Legal Safeguards
Exact Plot & Survey Number Ensure exact plot dimensions, survey number, and facing direction are spelled out.
Milestone-Based Payment Schedule Payments tied to measurable development progress rather than arbitrary calendar dates.
Possession & Registration Deadline Definite handover date with penal compensation clauses for developer delays.
Clear Refund & Cancellation Terms Explicit clause permitting full advance refund if title defects arise during legal audit.

Conclusion

Buying farmland can be a rewarding step when the property matches your goals and the legal documentation is in order. Following this seven-point checklist helps you evaluate ownership, land classification, boundaries, connectivity, amenities, costs, and seller credibility before making a commitment.

Take the time to inspect the property, obtain independent legal advice, and verify every important claim. A careful approach can help you avoid preventable problems and make a better-informed property decision.
