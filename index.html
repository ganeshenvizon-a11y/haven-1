Key Corridors & Landmarks

  • NH-44 Bangalore Express Corridor

    Fast 6-lane National Highway connecting Hyderabad directly to Bengaluru. Uninterrupted, smooth drive from the city.

  • Proposed Regional Ring Road (RRR)

    Government proposed RRR passes right through Gunded, Balanagar Mandal—driving massive regional infrastructure and property appreciation.

  • RGIA Airport & Outer Ring Road

    Direct straight-line highway access to Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (Shamshabad) and the ORR network connecting to Gachibowli.

  • Shadnagar Economic Hub

    Close to corporate giants like P&G, Johnson & Johnson, DLF, Adani Data Center, and Symbiosis International University.

  • Balanagar Station & Gunded Road

    Balanagar Railway Station on the Hyd-Kurnool line; smooth scenic blacktop road from Balanagar via Gunded straight to Nerellapalli.
